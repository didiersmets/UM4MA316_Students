but in (1 - 10^20)*x2 = 2 - 10^20 these two numbers get rounded up to 1 by the computer

the eq are eps*x1 + x2 = 1 and x1 + x2 = 2, being eps = 10^-20
exact solution:
    x1 = 1/(1 - eps)
    x2 = (1 - 2*eps)/(1 - eps)
nearly (1, 1)

computer solution:
    eps is the 1st pivot, so 1/eps = 10^20,
    so the new diagonal entry is u22 = 1 - 10^20.
    forward substitution gives y1 = 1 and y2 = 2 - 10^20.
    1 - 10^20 and 2 - 10^20 differ by 1 and both round to -10^20.
    backward substitution gives x2 = y2/u22 = (-10^20)/(-10^20) = 1.
    and the first equation x1 = (1 - x2)/eps = 0.
so the computed solution is  (0, 1)
